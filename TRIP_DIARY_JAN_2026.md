**Family:** Andrew, Sharmila, Taj (10), Maya (8)

## January 1 (Wednesday) — Kanazawa, The Lian

**Weather:** Snowy

### Where We Stayed
The Lian Kanazawa — boutique hotel on Kanazawa Omotesando, 1 minute from Ōmi-chō Market. The hotel collaborates with local artists and galleries.

### Highlights
- Noticed the beautiful **naguri** (名栗) flooring in the hotel — traditional hand-carved wood technique using a chōna adze. Each scallop mark made individually by hand.
- Explored The Lian's curated restaurant list
- Discussed dinner options — kids were tired and wanted Italian
- **Dinner**: Likely Aipini (Italian, 3 min walk) or Ushijyu Teraoka (Noto beef, 2 min)

### Planning
- Mapped out coffee spots near the hotel: Curio Espresso, Higashide Coffee, Townsfolk Coffee
- Researched Kanazawa Loop Bus for getting around
- Planned next day's activities

## January 2 (Thursday) — Final Day in Kanazawa → Tokyo

**Weather:** Snowy afternoon

### Morning/Midday
- Breakfast options explored: Ōmi-chō Market grazing, Hippo Bakery (かばのパンや), Curio Espresso
- Visited **21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art** — SANAA architecture, Swimming Pool installation by Leandro Erlich

### Lunch
- **Kenrokutei** — historic restaurant (est. 1918) overlooking Japan's oldest fountain
- Ordered: **Jibuni** (治部煮) — Kanazawa's signature duck stew with seasonal vegetables. Also tried the Jibusoba set.
- The restaurant's tatami room overlooks Kenrokuen Garden

### Afternoon
- Snowy afternoon sightseeing (had already visited Kenrokuen Gardens and Nagamachi Samurai District earlier in trip)
- Recommended: **D.T. Suzuki Museum** — Yoshio Taniguchi architecture, contemplative water garden beautiful in snow

### Evening
- **Last dinner in Kanazawa** — reservation made (possibly TILE in the 110-year-old machiya, or Ichirin for kaiseki)
- Travel to Tokyo via Shinkansen (Kagayaki, ~2h30m)

## January 3 (Friday) — Arrival Tokyo / Odaiba Exploration

### Morning — Tokyo Station (8:30am arrival)
- Arrived Tokyo Station with luggage from Kanazawa
- **Breakfast**: Rokumei Coffee (Gransta Marunouchi, near North Exit)
  - First Tokyo branch of famous Nara roaster (opened July 2024)
  - Morning set: ham & cheese croissant + latte (¥920)
  - Luggage storage available, many seats, power outlets

### Late Morning — Odaiba
- Took Yurikamome monorail to Odaiba (sat in front row — no driver, glass front, kids loved it)
- Dropped bags at **Hilton Tokyo Odaiba** (they hold luggage before 3pm check-in)

### Midday
- **Miraikan** (National Museum of Emerging Science and Innovation)
  - ASIMO robot demos, Geo-Cosmos globe
  - Interactive science — good for kids

### Lunch
- **DiverCity Tokyo Plaza** — Tokyo Gourmet Stadium food court (2F)
- Options: Kaneko Hannosuke tendon, Hamburg & Steak HIRO, Kaiou conveyor sushi

### Afternoon
- Explored DiverCity — Giant Gundam statue photo op
- Possibly Unko Museum (kids found it hilarious)
- **3:00pm**: Checked into Hilton Odaiba
  - Requested Rainbow Bridge view room
  - Explored hotel — noted balcony views of Tokyo Tower, Rainbow Bridge, Sky Tree

### Evening

## January 4 (Saturday) — teamLab Planets + MOT + Gift Shopping

### Morning — teamLab Planets (Toyosu)
- **10:00am entry** (booked in advance, ¥13,400 for family)
- Wore shorts/roll-up pants (knee-deep water in some rooms)
- Downloaded app beforehand for interactive features
- Highlights:
  - Koi pond — fish swim around your feet
  - Infinity mirror rooms
  - Flower universe — lay down on the floor for photos
- Kids held hands in dark rooms, got wet (brought spare clothes)
- Out by ~12:00pm

### Midday — Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo (MOT)
- Took metro from Toyosu to Kiyosumi-Shirakawa (~30 min)
- **Current Exhibition**: Sol LeWitt: Open Structure (Dec 25, 2025 – Apr 2, 2026)
  - First major survey in Japan
  - 6 wall drawings, structures, works on paper
  - Conceptual art pioneer — Sharmila appreciated this
- Kids free entry (under junior high school)
- Adults ¥1,600

### Afternoon — Kiyosumi-Shirakawa Coffee (possibly)
- Area known for specialty coffee:
  - Blue Bottle Coffee (roastery/flagship)
  - Arise Coffee
  - Allpress Espresso

### Late Afternoon — Back to Odaiba
- **Aqua City** for gift shopping
- Gift picks:
  - **Daiso**: Chopsticks, tabi socks, Japanese snacks, cute stationery (¥100 items)
  - **Le Edo** (1F): Nice chopsticks, Japanese crafts
  - **Kit Kats**: Matcha, sake, strawberry flavours
  - **Tokyo Banana**: Classic Tokyo souvenir
  - **Jump Shop**: Anime merch for kids' friends

### Evening (Planned)
- Rainbow Bridge illumination from hotel balcony
- Possibly Executive Lounge cocktail hour
- Rest before final day tomorrow
